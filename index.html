LM Private Drive

A private integration that connects an authorized leave-management application to a designated Google Drive account.

Its purpose is to store leave-proof documents, verify transferred files, and organize them by application date and approval status. Employees use the leave-management application; only the designated Drive account authorizes the Google connection.

The integration requests Google Drive access limited to files it creates or that are explicitly authorized for it. It uses the connected Google account’s identity to confirm that the correct account is connected.

This information site does not provide access to employee documents, account credentials, or the integration’s private source code.
